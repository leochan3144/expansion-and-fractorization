    Twelve question types from your worksheet: five on expanding with identities and seven on factorization. Write each line of working after the equals sign and earn the trophy.

    How it works

    • Each step is a new line starting with =. Type the whole line on the number pad.
    • Correct line: +10. A line with the wrong value: −10 and you get a hint.
    • If your line is equal but isn’t the step asked for, you get a tip and lose nothing. Steps marked “optional” can be skipped for both sets of marks.
    • Make a mistake and you’ll answer one more question of the same type. Finish a question with no mistakes to clear the type. Clear all 12 to win the trophy.
    • Brackets inside brackets show as [ ] like in your textbook. For powers, type the letter or bracket, press xn, then the number.
    • Sound is on. Use the speaker button at the top to turn it off.
    Your line

    No × needed between terms: 2(a)(8) works. The fraction key puts the next number underneath: x, fraction, 2 gives x over 2. Keyboard works too: letters, digits, + − * / ( ), ^ for powers, Enter to check.
